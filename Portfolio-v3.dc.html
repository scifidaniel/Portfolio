// ABOUT

I don't just make the video. I build the studio that makes it.

Five years across an international production house and an in-house startup studio. At Shuttlerock (NZ): short-form ads at volume — brand guidelines, platform specs, tight turnarounds, every aspect ratio. At The Umdenken (AU): founding creative hire. No studio, no pipeline, no process. I built post-production end to end, wrote it into the company's official SOP, and grew it into a four-person team I led.

That's the combination — the craft to make the work, and the systems thinking to make it repeatable after I've made it once. Sci-fi and HUD is where I'm most at home: every frame treated like an interface, precise grids, intentional glow, motion that reads as data made cinematic.
